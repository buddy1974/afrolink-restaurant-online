## Purpose
One-page digital menu website for **Afrolink Restaurant & Bar** (West African restaurant, Berzeliusstraße 7, 45144 Essen). It is the destination of the QR codes on Afrolink's printed menu cards.

Canonical URL: **https://www.afrolink-restaurant.online**

## Users
1. Guests at the table scanning the QR code (mobile; need the menu fast).
2. Locals searching for West African food in Essen (SEO, hours, contact, directions).

## In scope
Food menu · drinks menu · food gallery (genuine photos) · about · services · opening hours · contact (landline = official WhatsApp, plus mobile) · social links · local SEO + schema.org Restaurant.

## Never claim
Delivery (Afrolink does **not** deliver) · ratings, reviews, testimonials, awards · invented dishes or descriptions.

## Source of truth
Marcel's brief of 2026-10-07. Older printed menus (2025/2026) are **not** a source — they contain removed dishes and outdated prices.

## Design standard
Restrained editorial system: warm charcoal and warm paper, ochre/terracotta accent, burgundy sparingly, one geometric device (the lozenge rule). No gold gradients, glow, glassmorphism, stock food or emoji icons.
